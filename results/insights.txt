======================================================================
EXPERIMENT INSIGHTS: Dimensionality Reduction Comparison
======================================================================

RESEARCH QUESTION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
How do different dimensionality reduction methods (PCA, UMAP, Autoencoder)
affect the classification performance of neural networks on CIFAR-100?

METHODS COMPARED:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
• Raw: Original flattened images (3072-D)
• PCA: Linear reduction (512-D)
• UMAP: Nonlinear manifold learning (512-D)
• Autoencoder: Learned nonlinear reduction (512-D)

CLASSIFIERS TESTED:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
• ResNet-50 (pretrained on ImageNet - transfer learning)
• Transformer (trained from scratch)
• Autoencoder (trained from scratch)

RESULTS SUMMARY:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Best overall: ResNet + Raw (65.82%)

Raw:
  Avg Accuracy: 42.98%
  Avg Time: 5253.9s
PCA:
  Avg Accuracy: 26.57%
  Avg Time: 1202.8s
UMAP:
  Avg Accuracy: 6.42%
  Avg Time: 1323.4s
AE:
  Avg Accuracy: 25.69%
  Avg Time: 1396.9s
