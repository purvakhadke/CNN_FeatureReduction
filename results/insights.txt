======================================================================
EXPERIMENT INSIGHTS: PCA Impact on CIFAR-100 Classification
======================================================================

Research Question:
How does PCA dimensionality reduction affect the performance of different
classifier architectures (ResNet, Transformer, Autoencoder) on CIFAR-100?

Key Findings:
----------------------------------------------------------------------

1. ResNet:
   Raw accuracy: 65.33%
   PCA accuracy: 28.22%
   Accuracy change: -37.11%
   Training speedup: 51.67x
   → PCA significantly HURT accuracy (trade-off for 51.7x speedup)

2. Transformer:
   Raw accuracy: 34.76%
   PCA accuracy: 31.76%
   Accuracy change: -3.00%
   Training speedup: 2.34x
   → PCA maintained similar accuracy with 2.3x speedup

3. Autoencoder:
   Raw accuracy: 27.89%
   PCA accuracy: 20.02%
   Accuracy change: -7.87%
   Training speedup: 8.45x
   → PCA significantly HURT accuracy (trade-off for 8.5x speedup)

======================================================================
Conclusions:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Best model on raw data: ResNet (65.33%)
- Best model with PCA: Transformer (31.76%)
- Average accuracy change with PCA: -15.99%
- Average training speedup with PCA: 20.82x
