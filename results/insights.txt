======================================================================
EXPERIMENT INSIGHTS: PCA Impact on CIFAR-100 Classification
======================================================================

RESEARCH QUESTION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
How does PCA dimensionality reduction (3072-D → 512-D) affect the
classification performance of ResNet, Transformer, and Autoencoder
architectures on CIFAR-100?

KEY FINDINGS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------

1. ResNet:
   Raw accuracy:     65.33%
   PCA accuracy:     28.22%
   Accuracy change:  -37.11%
   Training speedup: 51.67x
   → Significant accuracy loss; PCA may remove important features

2. Transformer:
   Raw accuracy:     34.76%
   PCA accuracy:     31.76%
   Accuracy change:  -3.00%
   Training speedup: 2.34x
   → Moderate accuracy trade-off for 2.3x speedup

3. Autoencoder:
   Raw accuracy:     27.89%
   PCA accuracy:     20.02%
   Accuracy change:  -7.87%
   Training speedup: 8.45x
   → Significant accuracy loss; PCA may remove important features

======================================================================
CONCLUSIONS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
• Best model on raw data: ResNet (65.33%)
• Best model with PCA:    Transformer (31.76%)
• Average accuracy change with PCA: -15.99%
• Average training speedup with PCA: 20.82x

t-SNE INTERPRETATION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The t-SNE visualization shows how well-separated the class clusters are
in each feature space. More distinct, tight clusters indicate better
separability and potentially easier classification.
